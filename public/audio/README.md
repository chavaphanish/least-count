# YAYDU original sound effects

These short mono MP3s are original procedural sound effects generated for this project by `scripts/generate-audio.py`: filtered-noise card Foley, an additive bell, a short clock click, and a layered crowd of palm-clap transients with short reflections. No commercial recordings or third-party samples are used. They may be used, modified and distributed with YAYDU. No background music or voice is included.

Regeneration requires Python 3 and ffmpeg with libmp3lame, not additional app dependencies. Durations: card effects 0.12–0.22s, bell 0.8s, tick buffer 1s (one click per loop), round applause 3s, game applause 5s. MP3 encoder padding is trimmed by browser decoding.
